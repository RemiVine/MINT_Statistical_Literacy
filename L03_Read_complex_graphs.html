Lecture 3 Challenge: Reading Complex Inequality Graphs

Interpret levels, shares, growth rates, crossings and distributional patterns — without over-interpreting what a graph can establish.

Graph-reading guide

Read the axes first. Is the graph showing levels, shares or growth rates?
Shares are not levels. A falling share does not necessarily mean falling absolute income.
Look for structure. Crossings, peaks and troughs often carry the main message.
Description is not causation. A graph alone rarely identifies the causal mechanism.
